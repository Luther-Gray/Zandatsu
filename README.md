Zandatsu is a Hyper-Destructive Blockbench Plugin that cuts through meshes along a selected angle. Perfect for shaving down rough objects like rocks and cliffs.

[Zandatsu.webm](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/15bf0ec1-41c2-4184-83e6-a14f5b404cd5)


The Zandatsu Tool works similarly to a boolean modifier from Blender or M-Tools. It creates a line that stretches across the screen and is controlled by moving your mouse clockwise or counter-clockwise. Anything to the **LEFT** (Configurable in Toolbar) of the line, in the slightly red area, is cut off and deleted.

After being cut off, the edges are then capped, keeping your geometry solid and trianglized(?).

Move your mouse to slide the Zandatsu -> Click to *Lock* in the Slide -> Move your mouse to rotate Zandatsu -> Click to Cut!

Right Click to cancel Zandetsu's Lock.

Be careful! This tool can affect multiple meshes all at once! Keep your camera (F)ocused using Blockbench's **Center View on Selection** Keybind to make your cuts easier.
