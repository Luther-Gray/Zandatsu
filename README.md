# 🔪 Zandatsu
Zandatsu is a Blockbench Plugin that cuts through meshes along a selected angle. Perfect for shaving down rough objects like rocks and cliffs.

The Zandatsu Tool works similarly to a boolean modifier from Blender. It creates a line that stretches across the screen and is controlled by moving your mouse clockwise or counter-clockwise. Anything to the **LEFT** (Configurable in Toolbar) of the line, in the slightly red area, is cut off and deleted.

Move your mouse to slide the Zandatsu -> Click to Lock in the slide -> Move your mouse to rotate Zandatsu -> Click to Cut!

Right Click to cancel Zandetsu's Lock.

Be careful! This tool can affect multiple meshes all at once! Keep your camera (F)ocused using Blockbench's **Center View on Selection** Keybind to make your cuts easier.
